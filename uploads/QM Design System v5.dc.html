QM Design System — v5
Illuminated narrative observatory
v5 covers every surface — the writing room, the scene room, compose, the configurator and the outline — on one semantic foundation, plus the shared parts they are all built from. New in v5: audience (in-scene vs private) is expressed as surface temperature, not another accent.
PRINCIPLE 01
Illuminate what matters now
Selection, current location, conflict and active editing receive light. Everything else recedes.
PRINCIPLE 02
Let authored material breathe
Narrative content gets size, warmth and rhythm; structural metadata stays subordinate but legible.
PRINCIPLE 03
Colour explains, never decorates
Every accent identifies state, type, location or consequence — and is always paired with a shape.

Signal layers — what each visual property owns
Material state and selection are different dimensions, so they never share a property. A beat can be proposed and unselected, canonical and selected, or proposed and focused, and each reads correctly.

Composition — how signals layer
The screen above carries four signals at once: the beat is proposed, selected, concerns Sarita, and offers a canon transition. These rules keep that legible as screens get harder.
01Validation overrides every other accent while it blocks. A conflict outranks proposed, suggested and selection.
02Focus sits outside the component and never replaces state — a focused proposed beat keeps its amber rail.
03Selection changes surface, not identity. An unselected proposed beat keeps its hollow dot and chip, and gets no rail.
04Material state controls the rail and the state glyph, in every view, at every depth.
05Entity and location signals stay local to their own tokens — an avatar, a linked name, a HERE flag. They never colour a container.
06Hover may brighten a surface. It may never introduce semantic colour.

RULES
One filled action per region — Save globally, Apply in the inspector, nothing filled on the beat card. Icons 16px at 1.5px stroke; status glyphs 8px. Truncate to two lines with the full title on hover, never one-line ellipsis. Motion 120–200 ms ease-out, and none at all under reduced-motion.
Persistent size floor: 14px for sans body and rows, 12.5px for mono chronology and module labels. Below that, the information moves to hover or selection — the same test as contrast.

Interaction layer The outline builder gates by withholding a handler, not by disabling a control — so every refusal must say why, and name the key that works.
Keys are a token, refusals are a pattern
A refusal is not an error. It is the contract explaining itself, so it uses structural grey and a key token — never conflict cinnabar, which stays reserved for material that is actually wrong.
