STACKING ORDER
Stacking is elevation seen from the side, so the two live together. QM has exactly two layers and the scale names only those: nothing here is sticky, and “saved to QM” is a lock, not a toast. Every other absolute element wins on paint order alone.
--qm-z-popover50 a panel floating over the content it is anchored to
--qm-z-scrim100 the modal layer — Overlay’s scrim, over the page
The 100 is not a bar a popover has to clear. The scrim is position:fixed with a z-index, so it is a stacking context of its own: a Picker opened inside a modal resolves its 50 against the panel, never against the scrim. Keep a popover inside the context that opened it — one portalled to the body while a modal is up leaves that context and vanishes behind the scrim.
