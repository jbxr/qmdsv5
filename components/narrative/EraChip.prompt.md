One-line: The mono chronology chip — story-time on a scene, a row, or inline in prose.

```jsx
<EraChip>Y−6 D1</EraChip>
<EraChip kind="timeless">timeless</EraChip>
<EraChip kind="peek">Y−40</EraChip>
<EraChip kind="teaching">Y3.100</EraChip>
```

Notes
- Use the minus sign − (U+2212) for negative years, as QM's own copy does.
- `timeless` is grey on purpose: no story-time is a fact, not a warning.
