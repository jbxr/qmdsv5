One-line: The italic serif aside that sits beside authored material — a note, a consult read, a stage direction.

```jsx
<NoteBlock>Note — he is describing the Chrysalis.</NoteBlock>
<NoteBlock tone="consult">~ vera reads along: he has not said her name yet</NoteBlock>
```

Notes
- Author notes accepted from a consult are written as `((double parentheses))` and are undoable like any edit.
- Never give a note a card surface or a border — the rule is the whole affordance.
- Every prop the block does not claim reaches the wrapping `<div>`, so `id`, `aria-*` and `data-*` behave natively.
- The rule, the well behind it and the ink are one row, so a `tone` outside the union renders as `neutral` whole. It used to take the neutral rule over a transparent well — none of the three tones this declares.
