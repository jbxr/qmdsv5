One-line: The message QM shows when a key press is withheld — what happened, why, and what to press instead.

```jsx
<Refusal pressed="⌫">3 rows sit under this one. <span>Use</span> <Kbd>⌘⌫</Kbd> <span>to delete the subtree.</span></Refusal>
<Refusal pressed="⌘S">Already saved — v1 is write-once. Open a new outline.</Refusal>
```

Notes
- Never cinnabar: a refusal is the contract explaining itself, not damaged material.
- A second refusal replaces the first; they never stack.
