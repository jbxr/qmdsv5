One-line: The bar that appears whenever a surface is in a mode, carrying the accent of the object it is waiting on.

```jsx
<ModeBar mode="linking">Linking — click a row to bind this event</ModeBar>
<ModeBar mode="inspect" exitKey="hold ⌥">Inspect — click a name to pin its dossier</ModeBar>
<ModeBar mode="peek" exitKey="reset">Peeking at Y−40 — the document anchor is Y35.100</ModeBar>
```

Notes
- A mode with no visible bar is a bug. Esc always exits, and the bar always names the way out.
